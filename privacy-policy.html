PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 18, 2026 • SolidGround Infrastructure & Construction

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At SolidGround Infrastructure & Construction ("SolidGround", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the proprietary technical, commercial, and personal information you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, storage, processing, and protection of information obtained through our website, Request for Proposal (RFP) tender submissions, and engineering consultation inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide heavy civil engineering services, bid estimates, and project management consultations:

  • Contact Details: Full name, professional email address, company affiliation, physical location, and direct phone number.
  • Tender & Project Specifications: Architectural drawings, CAD/DWG blueprints, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), geotechnical data, site coordinates, and project timelines submitted via our bid portal.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and anonymized access telemetry used to optimize website performance and ensure cybersecurity.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed solely for legitimate commercial engineering purposes:

  • Conducting pre-construction feasibility assessments and preparing formal EPC bid tenders.
  • Direct technical correspondence regarding machinery allocation, timeline logistics, and cost estimations.
  • Complying with municipal civil engineering regulations, OSHA safety standards, and statutory reporting obligations.

4. Data Security & Intellectual Property

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards to protect structural designs, commercial estimates, and personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All tender documentation submitted through our web portal is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS cryptographic protocols.

We maintain strict internal access controls; only certified senior estimators and project directors assigned to your prospective development are granted access to your project plans.

5. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing

SolidGround does not sell, lease, or monetize client data under any circumstances. Information may only be disclosed to vetted structural subcontractors or engineering consultants under binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) solely for the purpose of joint project delivery.

6. Contact Our Privacy Office

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data modification or deletion, please contact our legal department:
