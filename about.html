ABOUT US

// OUR STORY

BUILT ON TRUST, DRIVEN BY EXCELLENCE

Founded in 2006, SolidGround began as a specialized civil contracting firm dedicated to high-precision earthworks, foundational stabilization, and municipal site preparations.

Through rigorous engineering discipline and relentless capital reinvestment into heavy equipment, we grew into a full-scale heavy civil and structural infrastructure firm operating across the region.

Today, our multidisciplinary force of 1,200+ engineers, surveyors, and field operators has successfully delivered 650+ landmark projects with an uncompromising focus on structural longevity and safety.
